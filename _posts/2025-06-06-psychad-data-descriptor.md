The [PsychAD data descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04687-5) is out in *Scientific Data*, with Donghoon Lee as co-first author. It documents genotypes and single-nucleus transcriptomes from more than 6.3 million nuclei and 1,494 donors, covering neurodegenerative disease, serious mental illness and neurotypical controls. About a fifth of the donors (21%) had two or more major brain disorders, so the data are well suited to studying how diseases overlap.

The data are available through the [AD Knowledge Portal](https://doi.org/10.7303/9618136), and the atlas can be browsed on [CELLxGENE](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/collections/84ce6837-548d-4a1f-919f-0bc0d9a3952f).
