[Plasticity of human microglia and brain perivascular macrophages in aging and Alzheimer's disease](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02716-6) is now online in *Nature Genetics*.

We profiled 832,505 myeloid cells from the prefrontal cortex of 1,607 donors and sorted them into 6 subclasses and 13 subtypes. A disease-associated microglia subtype marked by high GPNMB expands as Alzheimer's pathology worsens. It carries a lot of the disease's genetic risk and is more phagocytic, MITF keeps cells in that state, and its protective effect depends on TREM2.

Donghoon Lee is first and co-corresponding author, sharing first authorship with James Vicari and two of our graduate students, Christian Porras and Collin Spencer. Code: [DiseaseNeuroGenomics/scMyeloidAD](https://github.com/DiseaseNeuroGenomics/scMyeloidAD).
