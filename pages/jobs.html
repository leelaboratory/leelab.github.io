Why join us

Important questions.
Room to make them yours.

How do cells become vulnerable to disease? Why do some people remain cognitively resilient? Which genetic changes matter, and in which cells? Our datasets open the door to questions that span the full complexity of the human brain.

At the Lee Lab, you’ll work at the intersection of computational methods and biological discovery, within the collaborative Center for Disease Neurogenomics at Mount Sinai.

Your research environment

Build the skills to ask more.

01

Data with depth

Work with population-scale human brain data: 6.3 million nuclei in PsychAD and more than 832,000 immune cells in FreshMG.

02

Methods that matter

Develop expertise in single-cell analysis, statistical genetics, machine learning and reproducible computational research.

03

A connected community

Collaborate across the Center for Disease Neurogenomics, with access to Mount Sinai’s research community and Minerva computing resources.
